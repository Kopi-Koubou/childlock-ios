# Childlock Brand Guide

Childlock is a calm, trustworthy family tool. It turns interruptions to a
child's screen time into short brain breaks without presenting the parent as a
punisher or the child as a problem to control.

## Design posture

Use warm bone canvases, white grouped surfaces, forest-sage actions, restrained
terracotta attention states, large readable child interactions, and native iOS
patterns. Parent screens should feel competent and compact. Child screens should
feel sparse, encouraging, and easy to understand at a glance.

The app icon is the primary brand mark. Preserve its sage background, bone
shield, and forest lock. Do not invent a wordmark.

## Product truth

Childlock runs on the device where setup is completed. It does not ship with a
remote parent-phone dashboard. The launch build targets iPhone and iPad on iOS
17+, uses Sign in with Apple as the visible authentication path, and keeps
Screen Time enforcement available without Premium.

The enforced brain break runs directly on the system Screen Time shield as one
question with two answer choices. A correct answer briefly shows “Great job!”,
then clears the shield and reveals the already-open content automatically. The
child has no post-answer button or return gesture.

## Content posture

Write plainly. Explain system limits before the user encounters them. Use
“Brain Break”, “Great job!”, and “Almost! Try again.” Avoid fear, surveillance,
addiction, punishment, remote-control promises, rankings, streaks, and challenge
bypasses.

## Implementation boundary

Open Design prototypes are design explorations. Accepted directions must be
translated into the production SwiftUI token and component system, then
validated on iPhone, iPad, Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion, VoiceOver, and physical
Screen Time hardware flows.
